# Viva Preparation

Status: scaffold only.

For every implemented component, add:

- Objective, variables, constraints, and baseline in plain language.
- Why the method applies and what assumptions it needs.
- A five-minute walkthrough of the implementation.
- One safe modification that can be performed without AI.
- Correctness oracle/invariant and known failure modes.
- Interpretation of the measured result without overstating causality or speedup.
